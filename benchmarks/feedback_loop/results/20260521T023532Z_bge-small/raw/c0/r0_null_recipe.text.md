### 25-Minute Honey Garlic Chicken & Pepper Stir-Fry

This recipe is perfect for a quick weeknight meal, utilizing the juicy flavor of chicken thighs and the sweetness of a single bell pepper.

**Prep time:** 10 mins | **Cook time:** 15 mins | **Total time:** 25 mins

#### Ingredients
*   **1 lb (450g)** boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
*   **1** bell pepper (any color), sliced into thin strips
*   **1** small onion, sliced (optional)
*   **2 cloves** garlic, minced
*   **1 tbsp** vegetable oil
*   **Serve with:** Steamed rice or noodles

**The Sauce:**
*   **2 tbsp** soy sauce
*   **1 tbsp** honey
*   **1 tsp** fresh ginger, minced (or ½ tsp ground ginger)
*   **½ tsp** red pepper flakes (optional, for heat)

#### Instructions
1.  **Sear the Chicken:** Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the chicken pieces in a single layer. Let them sear undisturbed for 3 minutes to get a nice brown crust, then toss and cook for another 3-4 minutes until nearly cooked through.
2.  **Sauté Vegetables:** Add the sliced bell pepper and onion to the skillet. Stir-fry for 3-5 minutes until the pepper is vibrant and "crisp-tender" (softened but still has a bite).
3.  **Glaze:** Stir in the garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the soy sauce, honey, ginger, and red pepper flakes. 
4.  **Finish:** Toss everything together for 1-2 minutes. The sauce will bubble and thicken slightly, coating the chicken and peppers in a glossy glaze.
5.  **Serve:** Remove from heat immediately and serve over a bed of warm rice.

**Pro Tip:** For extra crispy chicken, toss the raw chicken pieces in 1 tablespoon of cornstarch before searing.
