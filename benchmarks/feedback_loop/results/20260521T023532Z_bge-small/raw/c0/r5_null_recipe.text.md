Here is a quick, flavorful 30-minute recipe for **Balsamic Chicken and Bell Pepper Skillet**:

### Ingredients
*   **1 lb Chicken Thighs:** Boneless and skinless, cut into bite-sized pieces.
*   **1 Bell Pepper:** Any color, sliced into strips.
*   **1/2 Onion:** Sliced (optional, but adds great flavor).
*   **2 cloves Garlic:** Minced.
*   **2 tbsp Balsamic Vinegar.**
*   **1 tbsp Olive Oil.**
*   **Seasoning:** Salt, black pepper, and 1/2 tsp dried oregano (or Italian seasoning).

### Instructions
1.  **Sear the Chicken (8-10 mins):** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season the chicken pieces with salt, pepper, and oregano. Add them to the skillet and cook until browned on all sides and nearly cooked through.
2.  **Sauté Vegetables (5-7 mins):** Add the bell pepper strips and onions to the pan. Sauté until the vegetables are tender-crisp and have some light charring.
3.  **Aromatics (1 min):** Stir in the minced garlic and cook for about 60 seconds until fragrant.
4.  **Glaze (2-3 mins):** Pour the balsamic vinegar into the skillet. Use a wooden spoon to scrape up any browned bits (fond) from the bottom of the pan. Let the vinegar simmer and reduce until it forms a glossy glaze that coats the chicken and peppers.
5.  **Serve:** Taste and add more salt or pepper if needed. Serve immediately over rice, quinoa, or with crusty bread.

**Tip:** If you like a bit of heat, add a pinch of red pepper flakes when you add the garlic.
