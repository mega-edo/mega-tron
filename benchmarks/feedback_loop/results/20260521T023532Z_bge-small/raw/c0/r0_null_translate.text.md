The formal Korean translation for "Hello, how are you?" is:

**안녕하세요? 어떻게 지내세요?**
*(An-nyeong-ha-se-yo? Eo-tteo-ke ji-nae-se-yo?)*

For a more formal or professional setting (e.g., addressing a superior or a large audience), you can use:

**안녕하십니까? 어떻게 지내십니까?**
*(An-nyeong-ha-sim-ni-kka? Eo-tteo-ke ji-nae-sim-ni-kka?)*
