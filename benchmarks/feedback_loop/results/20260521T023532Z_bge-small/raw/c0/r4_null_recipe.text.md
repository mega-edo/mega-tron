Here is a quick, flavorful **30-minute One-Pan Lemon Garlic Chicken and Peppers** recipe.

### Ingredients
*   **1 lb Chicken Thighs** (boneless, skinless preferred for speed), cut into bite-sized pieces
*   **1 Bell Pepper**, sliced into strips
*   **2 cloves Garlic**, minced
*   **1 tbsp Olive Oil**
*   **1 tsp Dried Oregano** (or Italian seasoning)
*   **1/2 Lemon** (juice and zest)
*   **Salt and Black Pepper** to taste

### Instructions
1.  **Prep:** Season the chicken pieces with salt, pepper, and oregano.
2.  **Sear Chicken:** Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until browned and cooked through (about 6–8 minutes). Remove chicken from the pan and set aside.
3.  **Sauté Pepper:** In the same skillet, add the bell pepper strips. Sauté for 3–4 minutes until they are tender-crisp and have some char marks. 
4.  **Combine:** Add the minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Return the chicken to the pan.
5.  **Finish:** Stir in the lemon juice and zest. Toss everything together for 1 minute to meld the flavors.
6.  **Serve:** Great over rice, pasta, or with a piece of crusty bread.

**Pro-tip:** If you have some red pepper flakes or a splash of chicken broth, add them in step 5 for extra heat or a light pan sauce.
