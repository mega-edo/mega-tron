**Quick Lemon Garlic Chicken & Pepper Skillet (30 mins)**

1. **Prep:** Slice 1 lb boneless chicken thighs and 1 bell pepper into thin strips.
2. **Sear:** In a large skillet over medium-high heat, brown chicken in olive oil until cooked through (8–10 mins). Remove chicken.
3. **Sauté & Finish:** Add peppers to the same pan; cook until tender-crisp (3–5 mins). Return chicken to pan with 2 cloves minced garlic, a squeeze of lemon juice, and salt/pepper. Toss to combine and serve over rice or crusty bread.
