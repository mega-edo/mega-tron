### Honey Soy Chicken & Pepper Stir-Fry (25 Minutes)

**Ingredients:**
* **1 lb chicken thighs**, cut into bite-sized pieces
* **1 bell pepper**, sliced into strips
* **Aromatics:** 2 cloves garlic (minced) and 1 tsp ginger (minced)
* **Sauce:** 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, 1 tsp rice vinegar (or lime juice)
* **Cooking oil:** 1 tbsp vegetable or canola oil

**Instructions:**
1. **Sear Chicken:** Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken with salt/pepper and cook until browned and cooked through (6–8 mins). Remove chicken from the pan.
2. **Sauté Pepper:** In the same pan, add the bell pepper. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes until tender-crisp. 
3. **Fragrance:** Add garlic and ginger; cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
4. **Glaze:** Return the chicken to the pan. Pour in the soy sauce, honey, and vinegar. Toss for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats everything.
5. **Serve:** Best served immediately over steamed rice or noodles.
