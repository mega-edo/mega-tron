Here is a quick, 30-minute recipe for **One-Pan Honey Garlic Chicken & Peppers**:

**Ingredients:**
*   **Chicken:** 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces.
*   **Vegetable:** 1 bell pepper, sliced into strips.
*   **Sauce:** 3 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 2 cloves minced garlic.

**Instructions:**
1.  **Sear:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken with salt and pepper, then brown in the skillet for 6–8 minutes until cooked through.
2.  **Sauté:** Add the bell peppers to the skillet. Sauté for 3–4 minutes until tender-crisp.
3.  **Glaze:** Whisk the soy sauce, honey, and garlic together. Pour over the chicken and peppers. Toss for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens into a glossy glaze.
4.  **Serve:** Serve immediately over rice or noodles.
