Here is a simple, 30-minute **Honey Garlic Chicken & Bell Pepper Stir-Fry**:

### Ingredients
*   **1 lb Chicken Thighs**, cut into 1-inch bite-sized pieces
*   **1 Bell Pepper**, sliced into thin strips
*   **Sauce:** 3 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1 tsp grated ginger (optional)
*   **Cooking Oil**, Salt, and Pepper
*   **Optional:** Cooked rice or noodles for serving

### Instructions
1.  **Prep:** Season chicken pieces with salt and pepper. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, honey, garlic, and ginger.
2.  **Sear Chicken:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add chicken in a single layer. Cook for 5–7 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden brown and cooked through. Remove chicken from the pan and set aside.
3.  **Sauté Pepper:** Add another splash of oil if needed. Toss in the bell pepper strips and sauté for 3–4 minutes until crisp-tender.
4.  **Combine:** Return the chicken to the skillet. Pour the sauce over the mixture.
5.  **Thicken:** Simmer for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats everything in a glossy glaze.
6.  **Serve:** Serve immediately over rice or noodles.
