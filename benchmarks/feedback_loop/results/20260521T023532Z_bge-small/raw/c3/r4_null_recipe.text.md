Chicken thighs and bell pepper are a great combination for a quick weeknight meal. Here is a recipe for **Sheet Pan Chicken and Pepper Fajitas**, which takes about 30 minutes from prep to table.

### Ingredients
*   **Chicken:** 1 lb chicken thighs (boneless, skinless), sliced into strips.
*   **Vegetable:** 1 bell pepper (any color), deseeded and sliced into strips.
*   **Pantry:** 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp chili powder, ½ tsp cumin, ½ tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper.
*   **Serving (Optional):** Flour tortillas, lime wedges, or a side of rice.

### Instructions
1.  **Preheat:** Set your oven to 425°F (220°C).
2.  **Season:** On a large rimmed sheet pan, toss the chicken strips and bell pepper slices with olive oil and all the spices until evenly coated. Spread them out in a single layer.
3.  **Roast:** Bake for 18–20 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through (internal temp 165°F) and the peppers are slightly charred at the edges.
4.  **Serve:** Squeeze fresh lime juice over the top and serve immediately in warm tortillas or over rice.

**Tip:** If you prefer a stovetop version, you can sauté the chicken in a skillet over medium-high heat for 5-7 minutes, add the peppers for another 4-5 minutes, and follow the same seasoning steps.
