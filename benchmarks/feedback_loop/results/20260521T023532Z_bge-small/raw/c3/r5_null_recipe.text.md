I recommend a **Honey Garlic Chicken & Bell Pepper Stir-Fry**. It is optimized for a single bell pepper and quick weeknight preparation.

### **Ingredients**
*   **Chicken:** 1 lb chicken thighs (boneless/skinless), cut into bite-sized pieces.
*   **Veggie:** 1 bell pepper, sliced into strips.
*   **Aromatics:** 2 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp ginger (optional).
*   **Sauce:** 3 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 1 tbsp rice vinegar (or lime juice).

### **Instructions (30 Minutes)**
1.  **Prep Sauce:** Whisk the soy sauce, honey, and vinegar in a small bowl.
2.  **Sear Chicken:** Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add chicken thighs. Brown for 5–7 minutes until cooked through. Remove chicken from the pan and set aside.
3.  **Sauté Pepper:** In the same pan, add another splash of oil if needed. Toss in the sliced bell pepper. Sauté for 3–4 minutes until tender-crisp (don't overcook).
4.  **Combine:** Add the garlic and ginger to the peppers; cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. 
5.  **Glaze:** Return the chicken to the pan. Pour the sauce over everything. Stir constantly for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken and peppers.
6.  **Serve:** Best served over steamed rice or noodles.
