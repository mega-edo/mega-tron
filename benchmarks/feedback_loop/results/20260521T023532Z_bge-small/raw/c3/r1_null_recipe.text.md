### Chicken & Pepper Stir-Fry (30-Minute Meal)

**Strategy:**
*   **Prep (10 mins):** Slice chicken thighs into bite-sized pieces; de-seed and slice the bell pepper into strips.
*   **Cook (15 mins):** Sear chicken in a hot pan until browned, add the pepper and a quick sauce (soy sauce, ginger, garlic, and a touch of honey/sugar), and toss until the pepper is tender-crisp.
*   **Serve (5 mins):** Serve over quick-cook rice or noodles.
