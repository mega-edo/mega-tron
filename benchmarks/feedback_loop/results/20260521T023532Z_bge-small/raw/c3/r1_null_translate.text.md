The formal Korean translation for "Hello, how are you?" is:

**안녕하세요? 어떻게 지내십니까?** (Annyeong-haseyo? Eotteoke jinaesimnikka?)

Breakdown:
*   **안녕하세요?**: The standard formal/polite greeting for "Hello" (literally "Are you at peace?").
*   **어떻게 지내십니까?**: The formal/honorific way to ask "How are you?" or "How are you getting along?".

In a slightly less stiff but still polite context (Haeyo-che), you can say:
**안녕하세요? 어떻게 지내세요?** (Annyeong-haseyo? Eotteoke jinaeseyo?)
