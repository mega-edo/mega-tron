I recommend making a **30-Minute Garlic Ginger Chicken & Pepper Stir-Fry**. It’s fast, uses only one pan, and the chicken thighs stay juicy even at high heat.

### Ingredients
*   **1 lb (450g) Boneless, Skinless Chicken Thighs:** Cut into bite-sized pieces.
*   **1 Bell Pepper:** De-seeded and sliced into thin strips.
*   **Aromatics:** 3 cloves garlic (minced) and 1 tablespoon fresh ginger (grated).
*   **The Sauce:** 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey (or brown sugar), 1 tsp toasted sesame oil, and a splash of rice vinegar (optional).
*   **Cooking Oil:** 1 tbsp neutral oil (like vegetable or canola).

### Instructions
1.  **Prep the Sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, and vinegar. Set aside.
2.  **Sear the Chicken:** Heat the oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Add the chicken thighs in a single layer. Let them sear undisturbed for 3–4 minutes until golden brown, then flip and cook for another 2 minutes.
3.  **Sauté the Pepper:** Push the chicken to one side of the pan. Add the sliced bell pepper to the empty side. Stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until the pepper is crisp-tender.
4.  **Add Aromatics:** Add the minced garlic and grated ginger. Stir everything together for about 30 seconds until fragrant (be careful not to burn the garlic).
5.  **Glaze:** Pour the sauce over the chicken and peppers. Stir constantly for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats everything in a glossy glaze.
6.  **Serve:** Serve immediately over steamed rice, quinoa, or rice noodles. Top with sesame seeds or sliced green onions if you have them.

**Why this works:** Chicken thighs have more fat than breasts, which prevents them from drying out during the quick, high-heat sear, while a single bell pepper provides the perfect crunch and sweetness to balance the savory sauce.
