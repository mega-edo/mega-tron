For a quick and flavorful 30-minute weeknight dinner, here is a recipe for **Garlic-Soy Chicken Thighs with Bell Pepper**.

### Ingredients
*   **Chicken:** 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs (cut into bite-sized pieces)
*   **Vegetable:** 1 bell pepper (any color, sliced into strips)
*   **Aromatics:** 2 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tsp ginger (optional, minced)
*   **Sauce:** 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey or brown sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
*   **Pantry:** 1 tbsp cooking oil, salt, and pepper

### Instructions
1.  **Prep (5 mins):** Cut the chicken and slice the bell pepper. In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, honey, and sesame oil.
2.  **Sear Chicken (10 mins):** Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken with a pinch of salt and pepper. Add to the pan and cook until browned and nearly cooked through (about 5-7 minutes per side if whole, or 6-8 minutes total if cubed).
3.  **Sauté Pepper (5 mins):** Add the bell pepper strips to the skillet. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until they are tender-crisp.
4.  **Glaze (5 mins):** Stir in the garlic and ginger. Cook for 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the sauce mixture. Stir constantly for 1-2 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the chicken and peppers.
5.  **Serve:** Enjoy immediately over rice, noodles, or on its own.

**Total Time:** ~25-30 minutes.
